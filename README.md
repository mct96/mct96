### About

I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at the **Federal University of Minas Gerais (UFMG)** and a professor in the Informatics Department at the **Federal Institute of Mato Grosso (IFMT)**. My research sits at the intersection of **AutoML, evolutionary computation, and machine learning pipeline optimization** — I study how genetic programming, surrogate models, and fitness-landscape analysis can make automated machine learning faster, more interpretable, and more reliable.

I also work on applied machine learning and computer vision problems, including object detection for agriculture, and have collaborated on interdisciplinary studies spanning computational social science and health informatics.

- 🔭 Currently working on **deep learning** and **Pearl's causal inference framework**
- 🌱 Background in **differential equations and topology**, which inform my approach to optimization landscapes
- 🎓 M.Sc. in Computer Science · Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science (UFMG)
- 🏫 Teaching programming, algorithms, and computer science foundations at IFMT
- 📫 Reach me via [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mct1996/) or [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Matheus-Candido-Teixeira)
